### Built in Central India
"You don't have to leave Indore to do the biggest work of your life."
Anaxee is built in Indore and reaches every corner of Bharat. A National Startup Award, 350+ brands served, AI built in-house — all from Central India.

### How we work
1. You own real scope — Not a cog in a machine. You take a problem end to end, early.
2. Tech and AI first — We build our own apps, platforms and AI tools — reading field photos, cleaning data, catching fake visits.
3. Feet on the ground, data in the cloud — The rare place where deep tech meets the largest last-mile network in Bharat.
4. Mission over everything — Digitize Bharat is not a slide. It is the work, every day.

### What you get
1. Scope beyond your years — Own projects that touch lakhs of people.
2. Learn by building — Ship real AI tools, mobile apps and platforms at national scale.
3. Founders who have built — Learn from a team that has built ventures, earned a US patent and scaled across 26 states.
