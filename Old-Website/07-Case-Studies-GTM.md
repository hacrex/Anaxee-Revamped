## Go-To-Market (GTM)

**H1:** Put your product on the shelves of the next 500 districts.

**Intro:** Anaxee's GTM engine maps the market, profiles every retailer and takes orders on the ground — so brands, OEMs and D2C companies grow real distribution where it is hardest to reach.

CTAs: Plan your GTM · See the results

### The opportunity
"Most of India's demand is offline — and under-served."

- 88% of India lives in Tier 2/3/4 & rural
- 70% of household consumption is here
- 95% of commerce still happens offline
- $1.05T economy from these geographies

### What our GTM does
"More than data collection. We don't just hand you a list. We find the shops, tell you which ones matter, and start generating orders — all on one dashboard."

1. Market mapping — Discover, map and bring every target shop in a city or taluka onto a single dashboard — down to the street.
2. Retailer profiling (KYR) — Know Your Retailer — acquire, profile and estimate the revenue potential of each shop.
3. Order taking — Generate orders, push app adoption and sustain the relationship with regular visits — real sales, not just visibility.

### The operating model
"Brand controls. Anaxee executes. Distributor fulfils."

1. Brand controls — You own the message, schemes and pricing shown to the retailer.
2. Runner captures the order — Our Digital Runner meets the retailer and records the intent — the order — on the app.
3. Distributor fulfils — The order routes to the local distributor for delivery and payment collection.
4. Zero channel conflict — Distributors get orders without sales effort; you get 100% visibility of retailers.

### Why it is different — The new equation
| Traditional supply chain | With Anaxee |
|--------------------------|-------------|
| Limited visibility of retailers to the brand | 100% visibility of retailers to the brand |
| Distributor hires its own feet-on-street | No need to hire your own feet-on-street — activate ours on demand |
| Tier 2/3 retailers go unreached | Extended reach to nearby Tier 2/3 retailers |
| Patchy, delayed data | Real-time, verified, on one dashboard |

### Beyond order-taking — Merchandising, promotion and audit
- Merchandising — Proper on-shelf placement — eye-level, prominently visible, not covered or damaged.
- Promotion — Paste posters and distribute promotional material across target shops.
- Auditing & survey — Check posters, shelf share, stacking and display, with photo proof.

### Credit-enabled distribution
"Orders, with no-cost retailer credit built in"

1. Brand paid in advance — Payment secured upfront — no waiting on distributor cycles.
2. Retailer gets 30-day credit — Lending partners extend no-cost credit; interest absorbed by the brand or Anaxee.
3. Distributor becomes C&F — Just stores and delivers on order — no sales effort, no credit risk.
