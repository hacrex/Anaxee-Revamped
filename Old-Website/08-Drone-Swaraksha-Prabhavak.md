## Drone Services (/drone-services/)

**H1:** Drone mapping and MRV, backed by boots on the ground

**Stats:** 2,000+ hectares mapped · High accuracy · Speed & efficiency · Scalability · Transparency

**Intro:** Large-scale drone surveys fused with Digital Runner ground-truth — for plantations, forestry, agroforestry and carbon projects that need MRV-grade accuracy, not just pretty pictures.

### The idea
"Drones see the canopy. Our runners verify the ground."

Drone imaging stitched into orthomosaics gives a consistent, auditable view of a plantation. Anaxee overlays walk-based data from its Digital Runners on the drone maps — validating stocking density, mortality and canopy health — so you get verification you can stand behind. Every orthomosaic is GPS-accurate and can be laid over the real satellite map, so a funder anywhere can open it and see exactly what is on the ground, point by point.

### How it works
1. Land assessment — Runners assess each plot — water source, fencing, irrigation, out-of-scope trees — on the ground.
2. Drone survey & flight — Planned drone flights capture high-resolution, geotagged imagery across the site.
3. Orthomosaic generation — Images are stitched into orthomosaics for a consistent, auditable canopy view.
4. Ground-air integration — Ground surveys overlaid on maps validate density, mortality and canopy health — MRV-grade.

### What you get — Deliverables you can audit
- Orthomosaic maps — Stitched, geo-referenced aerial maps of the full plantation.
- Tree status & polygon mapping — Per-tree status and polygon (geo-fence) boundaries of each plot.
- Geotagged raw images — GPS-embedded tree photos for traceable, transparent reporting.
- Tree profiles — Species, height, plantation year, drip facility and GPS, tree by tree.
- Land-assessment dashboard — Water source, fencing, irrigation and out-of-scope plantation, mapped.
- Live Metabase dashboards — The real status of the plantation, filterable and up to date.

### Where it fits — Built for climate, forestry and land
- Agroforestry & plantations — Monitor stocking, survival and growth across thousands of plots.
- Carbon project MRV — Audit-grade monitoring, reporting and verification for carbon credits.
- Forestry — Canopy, health and coverage at scale.
- Land & asset assessment — Verify land use, boundaries and on-ground assets with GPS proof.
- Agriculture & agri-commodity — Crop and acreage verification, plus supply-chain and CSR ground-truth.

CTAs: Discuss your programme · Watch the documentary

### The flagship proof
In 2021, as India opened COVID vaccination to every adult, rural India hit a wall — hesitancy, misinformation, and the practical maze of CoWIN. Anaxee answered it on the ground.

We deployed trained local Digital Runners to go house to house: counselling families one-on-one, busting myths in their own language, registering them on CoWIN, booking slots, and making sure they actually got vaccinated. Every visit was logged on the Anaxee app, and runners were paid for each verified vaccination — accountability built in.

## Prabhavak (/prabhavak/)

**H1:** Reach rural India through the people who already live there.

**Intro:** Prabhavak is Anaxee's hyperlocal amplification network — 40,000+ trained community members who share your brand's own content inside the WhatsApp groups, Instagram and Facebook feeds they are already part of.

CTAs: Talk to us · See how it works

**Stats:** 40,000+ Trained members · 540 Districts · 26 States · 11,000+ Pincodes

### What Prabhavak is
"Your content, carried by trusted local voices."

Prabhavaks are real community members across rural and semi-urban India — a neighbour, a kirana owner, a didi or bhabhi — not professional influencers. They share a brand's own content within the local networks they already belong to: WhatsApp groups, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

The content stays brand-owned. When a Prabhavak shares your post, the likes, shares, views and new followers land on your brand's own pages — not on a middleman influencer's account.

Because the message comes from a familiar local face, it carries a kind of trust a banner ad cannot buy.

### How it works — Four steps
1. You share your content — Give us the posts, videos or messages you want seen. It stays yours, start to finish.
2. We activate your districts — Pick the exact districts or talukas you want. We mobilise trained Prabhavaks on the ground there.
3. They share it locally — Prabhavaks post it across the WhatsApp groups and social feeds where they are already known and trusted.
4. You see the numbers — Track groups covered, people reached and engagement on a dashboard — district by district.
