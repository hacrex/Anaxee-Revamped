# Anaxee.com — Visual Identity Summary

## Mood
Corporate-technical, grounded, India-rural focused. Dark-header + clean white sections; teal as the single hero accent.

## Dominant colour
Teal `#5cc6d0` (primary) / `#38b5c1` (active) — used for every interactive element (buttons, links, nav underlines, form focus, accents).

## Backgrounds
- White (`#fff`)
- Light gray (`#f3f3f3`)
- Dark charcoal (`#292929` nav, `#212121` footer)

## Typography pairing
- Body: Poppins
- Display / headings: Barlow Condensed

## Imagery style
- Real photos of Digital Runners in Indian markets/villages
- Geotagged verification photos
- Dashboard screenshots
- India map graphics
- Service images: grayscale by default → colour on hover

## Responsive
Bootstrap-based. Breakpoints: 1536px · 1366px · 1200px · 1024px · 992px · 768px · 767px · 576px · 320px

## Recurring Hindi tagline
"Jahaan tak pahunchna hai, hum wahaan pehle se hain." (Wherever you need to reach, we are already there.)
