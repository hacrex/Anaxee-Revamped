## About Us

**H1:** India's Reach Engine / About Anaxee

**Intro:** Anaxee Digital Runners is India's largest on-demand last-mile outreach and data-collection network. India is an assisting economy, and we provide the last-mile assistance that helps businesses, institutions and social organisations scale faster, to the remotest parts of the country, on demand.

**Stats:** 40,000+ Digital Runners · 540 Districts · 26 States · 11,000+ Pincodes · 350+ Clients · 130+ Team (Indore HQ)

### Who we are
A tried-and-tested feet-on-street model, run on technology. Every Digital Runner is a son or daughter of the soil, a local field executive with confirmed presence in their city or village, who performs the physical visit and completes the task digitally on the Anaxee app.

Model: shared, mobile-friendly, on-demand, centrally managed, professionally run, data-driven, built on a 1:1 approach with back-office support.

Goal: become the de-facto channel for companies to scale to the next 500 districts of India and cater to the next 500 million customers, in India and for clients around the world.

### The integrity gap
"Carbon buyers want impact they can prove. Most projects cannot show it."

61% — The voluntary carbon market's value fell from 1.9B to 0.72B USD in 2023 as buyers lost confidence in credit integrity.

### Where Anaxee is different
- A real feet-on-street network — 40,000+ trained local field executives across 540+ districts — not sub-contracted, not seasonal.
- Technology built for the field — Offline data capture, geotagged photos, polygon mapping, tree counting and drone surveys feed one auditable record.
- Integrity by design — Projects run against Gold Standard and Verra methodologies, including VM0047 for agroforestry, with MRV-grade evidence end to end.
