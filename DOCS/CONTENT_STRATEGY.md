# Anaxee Platform — SEO, GEO & AI Content Strategy

## Overview

This document defines the content strategy for the unified Anaxee platform, covering traditional SEO, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and AI search visibility. The goal is to position Anaxee as the authoritative source for retail intelligence, AI data collection, climate & carbon, influence marketing, and last-mile execution in India.

### 1.2 On-Page SEO Requirements

#### Every Page Must Include

1. **Unique title tag** (50–60 characters) with primary keyword
2. **Meta description** (150–160 characters) with keyword and CTA
3. **H1 tag** (one per page) with primary keyword
4. **H2–H6 hierarchy** with supporting keywords
5. **Internal links** (minimum 3–5 per page)
6. **Image alt text** (descriptive, keyword-relevant)
7. **Schema markup** (appropriate type per page)
8. **Canonical URL** (self-referencing)
9. **Open Graph tags** (title, description, image)
10. **Twitter Card tags** (title, description, image)

#### URL Structure Rules

- Lowercase only
- Hyphens for separators
- Include target keyword
- Maximum 3 levels deep
- No stop words (a, the, and, or, etc.)
- No parameters or session IDs

### 1.3 Technical SEO

#### Crawlability & Indexability

- XML sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` (auto-generated)
- robots.txt allowing all crawlers
- No orphan pages (every page linked from at least one other page)
- Clean URL structure
- No redirect chains (max 1 redirect)
- No 404 errors (monitor and fix monthly)

#### Core Web Vitals Targets

| Metric | Target | Threshold |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) | < 2.0s | < 2.5s |
| FID (First Input Delay) | < 50ms | < 100ms |
| CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) | < 0.05 | < 0.1 |
| INP (Interaction to Next Paint) | < 150ms | < 200ms |

#### Mobile Optimization

- Mobile-first design
- Responsive images with srcset
- Touch-friendly navigation
- No horizontal scrolling
- Fast mobile load times (< 3s on 3G)

## 2. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Strategy

### 2.1 What is GEO?

GEO optimizes content for AI-powered search engines that synthesize answers from multiple sources. Unlike traditional SEO (ranking in blue links), GEO ensures Anaxee content is cited, referenced, and recommended by AI systems.

### 2.2 GEO Content Principles

1. **Entity-First Content** — Clearly define who Anaxee is, what it does, and its unique value
2. **Structured Answers** — Provide direct, concise answers to common questions
3. **Data-Rich** — Include specific numbers, metrics, and facts
4. **Authoritative Tone** — Position content as the definitive source
5. **Comprehensive Coverage** — Cover topics thoroughly to be the go-to resource
6. **FAQ Integration** — Include FAQ sections on key pages

## 8. Content Migration Content Plan

### 8.1 Blog Content Migration Priority

**High Priority (Migrate as-is):**
- Posts with organic traffic > 100/month
- Posts with backlinks > 5
- Posts ranking in top 10 for target keywords
- Recent posts (< 12 months old)

**Medium Priority (Rewrite + Migrate):**
- Posts with organic traffic 10–100/month
- Posts with outdated information
- Posts with good content but poor SEO

**Low Priority (Consolidate or Archive):**
- Posts with zero traffic in 12 months
- Duplicate or near-duplicate content
- Posts below 500 words with no value
- Outdated content with no SEO value

### 8.2 Redirect Strategy

- Preserve existing URLs where possible
- Map old URLs to new URLs
- Implement 301 redirects for all changed URLs
- Monitor 404 errors for 30 days post-migration
- Update internal links in migrated content
