# Anaxee Website Transformation

> Transforming Anaxee's digital presence into a unified, modern, and scalable marketing platform.

## Overview

This repository contains the planning, design, content, architecture, and implementation assets for the next-generation Anaxee web platform.

The objective is to consolidate Anaxee's existing digital properties into a single, cohesive experience that accurately represents the company's evolution into a technology-driven organization delivering solutions across Retail Intelligence, Climate & Carbon Projects, AI Data Services, Data Collection, Influence Marketing, and Last-Mile Execution.

## Existing Digital Properties

* **Corporate Website:** https://anaxee.com
* **Anaxee Blog:** https://blog.anaxee.com
* **Prabhavak Platform:** https://prabhavak.anaxee.com
* **Anaxee Tech (lovable):** https://anaxeetech.com

## Vision

Create a world-class digital platform that positions Anaxee as:

> **India's Reach Engine**

A technology-enabled network combining AI, data, climate intelligence, and 250,000+ Digital Runners to solve last-mile execution and intelligence challenges across India and beyond.

## Project Goals

### Brand Transformation

* Reflect Anaxee's current business capabilities.
* Present a unified brand identity.
* Improve visual consistency across all digital touchpoints.

### Unified Experience

* Consolidate multiple websites into a single ecosystem.
* Create seamless navigation and customer journeys.
* Integrate blog and knowledge content directly into the primary platform.

### Enterprise Positioning

* Showcase solutions, products, and impact.
* Highlight case studies and client success stories.
* Demonstrate technology capabilities and nationwide scale.

### SEO, GEO & AI Visibility

* Improve traditional search engine rankings.
* Optimize for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
* Enhance discoverability across AI-powered search platforms.

### Scalability

* Support future products and business verticals.
* Enable rapid content publishing.
* Maintain consistent design and development standards.
